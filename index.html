What it does

  • Trade log Entry, exit, size, thesis, and outcome captured on every position, PSE and FX in one register.
  • Position sizing calculator Risk-per-trade limits enforced before entry rather than rationalized after.
  • Risk & R-multiple tracking Stop distance, reward-to-risk, and result measured in R, not pesos alone.
  • Study modules Structured lessons and notes, so learning is recorded the same way trades are.
  • Performance analytics Win rate, average R, drawdown depth, and streaks computed from the log itself.

Stop 07How I Work

Old-fashioned fundamentals, modern tools.

Reliability

A shuttle that shows up at 4:50 for a 5:00 pickup, every day, is worth more than any marketing. Contracts renew on consistency, not promises.

Clean books

Every entity files on time and reconciles to the peso. Compliance is not overhead. It is what lets a business bid for enterprise and government work.

Documentation

If it matters, it is written down: SOPs, incident reports, contracts, corrective actions. Paper trails protect relationships and settle disputes before they start.

Honest pricing

Rates are built from fuel, maintenance, labor, downtime, and compliance costs. I will restructure a route to save a client money, but I will not underprice into failure.

A stitch in time saves nine.
I believe that solving small problems early prevents bigger problems later. Taking action at the right time saves time, effort, and resources.
